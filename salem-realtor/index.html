Buying and selling a home in Salem, Utah

Kyla Muir is a REALTOR® with Real Estate Essentials based in Santaquin, working Salem as part of her south Utah County specialty. Acreage and horse property are a particular focus.

Salem has kept something most of Utah County has traded away — space. Larger lots, real acreage, horse property, and a pond in the middle of town that anchors the whole community. Buyers move here specifically for that, and they'll pay for it.

Selling acreage or horse property is genuinely different from selling a subdivision home. The buyer pool is smaller but far more motivated, and they care about things a standard listing never mentions: water rights, irrigation shares, fencing, outbuildings, zoning, and whether the barn is actually usable.

A listing that just says "1.2 acres" and moves on is leaving money on the table. Kyla markets those properties to the buyers specifically hunting for them — and when you're buying, she knows what questions to ask before you're committed.
